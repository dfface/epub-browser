# EPUB Browser v1.11.2

发布日期 / Release date: 2026-08-18

## 中文

这是一个修复连续阅读模式标注可靠性的维护版本。

### 修复

- **连续阅读中的标注定位**：标注现按实际所属章节和章节内相对位置保存，不再因加载、移除相邻章节而显示到错误位置或丢失。
- **标注恢复**：在连续阅读和退出连续阅读后，标注能在对应章节正常恢复，不再出现误导性的“Please reload the chapter”提示。
- **旧标注自动修复**：对于使用旧位置格式且仍能按文本找到的标注，阅读器会自动重新锚定并保存正确的位置与章节归属。

### 兼容性

- 不需要迁移或额外配置。
- 旧标注会在其所在章节被加载并成功定位后自动更新。

## English

This maintenance release improves annotation reliability in continuous reading mode.

### Fixes

- **Annotation placement in continuous reading**: Annotations are now stored with their actual chapter and chapter-relative position, so loading or removing adjacent chapters no longer places them incorrectly or makes them disappear.
- **Annotation restoration**: Annotations restore in their correct chapter both during and after continuous reading, without the misleading “Please reload the chapter” message.
- **Automatic repair for existing annotations**: When an annotation stored with the older position format can still be found by its text, the reader reanchors it and saves the correct position and chapter ownership.

### Compatibility

- No migration or additional configuration is required.
- Existing annotations are updated automatically after their chapter is loaded and the text is successfully located.
